About Us

We help your brain switch off.

Dreamz started with a familiar feeling. You're in bed. The lights are off. And your brain won't stop talking.

We knew the science existed to help. Research shows the brain responds to subtle signals when they align with its current state. So we set out to turn that science into something you could actually wear to bed — a soft mask that listens to your body, works quietly in the background, and helps the brain follow its natural path into sleep.

We are a small team of engineers, neuroscientists, and designers who care about one thing: helping people sleep well, so they can live well.

Why We Exist

Not forcing sleep. Calming the brain so sleep can happen.

Our mission

To improve people's well-being by enabling better sleep — leading to better health, stronger focus, enhanced memory, and better daily performance. A core part of that is helping people quiet the constant chatter that keeps them awake at night.

Our vision

Modern life leaves many of us mentally overstimulated, unable to shut off racing thoughts at night. We combine neuroscience, wearable hardware, and accessible software to help people calm their minds, fall asleep more easily, and wake up restored.

What Guides Us

The principles behind every decision.

Science firstEverything we build is grounded in peer-reviewed sleep and neuromodulation research. No hype, no overpromising.
Personal by designEvery brain is different. Our platform learns from your responses and adapts to you, night after night.
Quiet technologyThe best sleep tech is the kind you forget is there. Dreamz works in the background while you rest.
Improve, not just trackKnowing you slept badly doesn't help you sleep better. We focus on actively supporting sleep, not just scoring it.
Safety & responsibilityClear intended-use boundaries and careful, responsible deployment of neuromodulation technology.
Accessible neuroscienceComplex science, made simple and intuitive — so better sleep isn't reserved for specialists.

The Team

Built by people who test it on themselves.

Dreamz is built by a multidisciplinary team spanning neuroscience, embedded hardware, machine learning, and product design. We prototype our own electronics, run our own signal-processing pipelines, and wear every version of the mask ourselves — at our desks, on trains, and through full nights of sleep.

That closeness to the product matters. Before anything reaches you, it has already been through hundreds of nights on our own heads.

We're growing. If calm technology and better sleep sound like problems worth working on, we'd love to hear from you.
