DREAMZ Progress update

We're closing in on March, and it's really happening.

Watch: inside the build

It's been a busy few months in the workshop, and we wanted to bring you along for it. Every prototype that's come off the bench since our last update has been chasing the same three things: something that feels good against your skin all night, something you don't have to think twice about using, and something that comes out of the wash looking brand new.

We're not there because we rushed it. We're there because we kept rebuilding the parts that weren't good enough yet.

Where we landed

01
Comfortable enough to forget you're wearing it
We reshaped the contact points and softened the strap tension after rounds of overnight wear testing, so it sits still without pressing anywhere.
02
Easy to use, no manual required
One button, one charge cable, one strap adjustment. If you can put on a hat, you can put on Dreamz.
03
Machine washable
The fabric shell now separates cleanly from the electronics, so it goes in with a normal load and comes out ready for another week of sleep.

Launching March 2027. That's the date we're building toward, and every test we run this month is in service of hitting it with a mask we're genuinely proud of.

We're excited. Genuinely. This is the part of building something where it stops being a sketch and starts being a real thing you can hold, wash, and wear. Thank you for sticking with us while we got it right.
