Why sleep changes

Why perimenopause and menopause disrupt sleep

During perimenopause, estrogen and progesterone fluctuate and then decline. Declining estrogen is associated with a narrower comfortable temperature range, which is linked to hot flashes and night sweats, and many women find it harder both to fall asleep and to get back to sleep.

  • Hot flashes and night sweats

    A sudden rise in body heat can pull you out of sleep, often in the early hours, leaving you awake long after it passes.

  • Waking at 2 or 3 a.m.

    Many women describe waking in the middle of the night and lying awake, even when they fell asleep easily.

  • A mind that will not settle

    Once you are awake, a busy mind can make it hard to slip back into sleep before the alarm.

Perimenopause sleep mask questions

Can a sleep mask help with perimenopause insomnia?

A regular sleep mask only blocks light. Dreamz is a smart sleep mask: it senses your night and delivers gentle, personalized neuromodulation at bedtime and when you wake, to help you fall asleep and get back to sleep faster. It is a wellness device, not a medical treatment.

Does Dreamz stop hot flashes or night sweats?

No. Dreamz does not cool you down or treat hot flashes. It logs your skin temperature through the night so you can see when hot flashes and night sweats happen, and it helps you settle back to sleep after they wake you.

Is Dreamz a medical treatment for menopause?

No. Dreamz is a wellness device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or medical condition. Talk to a healthcare professional about menopause symptoms and treatment options. Many women bring their Dreamz sleep data to those conversations.

What does the stimulation feel like?

Most people barely feel it. The mask sends a gentle electrical signal chosen by its algorithm for that moment of your night, then goes quiet while you sleep.
